# Retail Sales Analysis Using SQl
## project Overview
 This project analyses a sample retail sales dataset using MYSQL to explore sales performance,customer purchases,product categories and revenue trends.
## Tools Used
 - MYSQL
 - MYSQL workbench
 - SQL
 - GitHub
## SQL Skills Demonstrated
 - SELECT and WHERE
 - GROUP BY and ORDER BY
 - Aggregate functions:SUM,COUNT, and AVG
 - DISTINCT
 - Checking missing values and duplicate records
 - UPDATE Statements
 
## Project Objectives

 1. calculate total sales revenue.
 2. Identity revenue generated by different products.
 3. Analyse sales performance across cities.
 4. Examine monthly sales trends.
 5. Identity and standardise inconsistent category names
 6. Check for missing values and duplicate order IDs.
 
## Dataset
 The project uses a fictional retail sales dataset containing 10 sales records.
 ### Project Files
 - `Retail_sales_Analysis.sql` - SQL queries used for cleaning and analysis.
 - `retail_sales_cleaned.csv` - Retail sales dataset.

## Key Business Insights
  - Total sales Revenue : £3,630
  - Total Orders : 10
  - Dataset period : January to March 2025
  - Products Analysed : Laptop , Shoes, Phone ,T-Shirt, Headphones and Jeans
  - Cities Analysed : London, Manchester, Birmingham, Sheffield and Leeds
    
 ## Product Revenue Analysis
  -  Highest Revenue Product : Laptop (£1,700)
  -  Second Highest Revenue product : Phone (£1,400)
  -  Lowest Revenue product : Jeans (£80)
  -  Laptops and Phones together generated approximately 85.4% of totals sales revenue.
     
## City Sales Analysis
  -  Highest Revenue city :London (£1,760)
  -  Second Highest Revenue City : Manchester (£1,020)
  -  Lowest Revenue City : Sheffield (£170)
  -  London contributed approximately 48.5% of total sales revenue.
  -  Business Insight : London to Manchester were the strongest revenue - generating cities in this sample dataset
     
## Monthly Sales Analysis
  - January 2025 Revenue : £1,420
  - February 2025 Revenue : £240
  - March 2025 Revenue : £1,970
  - Highest Revenue Month : March 2025
  - Lowest Revenue Month : February 2025
  - Total Revenue Month : £3,630

 ## Business Insights
  - sales revenue decreased significantly in February.
  - March recorded the highest monthly revenue.
  - The business experienced a strong recovery in sales during March.

## Category Sales Analysis
  - Electronics Revenue : £3,200
  - Fashion Revenue : £430
  - Highest Revenue Category : Electronics
  - Electronics Contributed approximately 88.2% of total sales revenue

## Category Business Insights
  - Electronics was the highest revenue generating category
  - Fashion contributed approximately 11.8% of total_revenue
  - The business generated most of its revenue from Electronics Products.

## Customer Purchase Analysis
  - Highest spending customers : Daniel (£900) and Noah (£900)
  - Third Highest spending customer : John (£800)
  - Lowest Spending customer : Sophia (£60)
  - Total Customers Analysed : 10

## Customer Business Insights
  - Daniel and Noah were the highest-spending customers, each contributing £900 in sales
  - Together ,Daniel and Noah generated approximately 49.6% of total revenue.
  - John was the third- highest-spending customer, contributing £800
  - The business could explore loyalty rewards for its highest-spending customers
